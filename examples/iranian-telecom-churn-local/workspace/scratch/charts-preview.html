Multiplying risk by value does not justify more offers

Customer Value has no documented currency or construction. It is used only as a proxy, never as profit. At the same 120-contact capacity, risk × value captures virtually the same observed churn-value as risk alone, while doubling down on people who do not churn.

Eligibility changes the apparent targeting performance

Status codes have very different prevalence. If the operator confirms that status code 1 is the currently eligible population, overall test precision substantially overstates campaign performance. This is the recommendation’s main flip test.

There are 300 excess exact duplicate records (9.5% of the file), with no identifier to establish whether these are errors or indistinguishable customers. Keeping predictor-identical records together prevents train/test leakage. Counting unique test records once gives AUC 0.839 and 59.4% recall at 20% capacity, close to the main results; this is a weight sensitivity, not an independently validated new model.

Prioritize a service experiment and reserve the discount budget

Service investigation: contact complaint-coded customers who are still eligible, prioritizing score when capacity is tight. Resolve the actual problem and measure resolution; do not automatically attach a discount.

Offer: no routine offers yet. A proposed rule for noncomplainants with score ≥20% and Customer Value ≥200 selected 10 held-out records, none of whom churned. It adds cost without observed risk concentration. Treat any future offer arm as a bounded randomized experiment after validating pre-period margin and eligibility.

No paid action: remaining customers receive normal service and monitoring. Low usage alone does not justify a subsidy, especially where value is low or zero. Reassess fresh pre-period signals, unresolved complaints and expected contribution margin.
